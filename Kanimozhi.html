About Shih Tzu

The Shih Tzu is a small and affectionate dog breed that originated in Tibet and later became popular in China. It is known for its long, silky coat, expressive eyes, and friendly personality. Shih Tzus are loyal companions and make excellent family pets.

They are gentle, playful, and enjoy spending time with people of all ages. With regular grooming, a healthy diet, daily walks, and proper care, Shih Tzus can live a happy and healthy life for 10 to 16 years. Because of their loving nature and adaptability, Shih Tzus are one of the most popular pet dog breeds around the world.
